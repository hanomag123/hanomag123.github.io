How My "Pay After Approval" Model Works

  1. Step 1 — We Agree on a Fixed, Written Scope

    Every page, feature, and acceptance criterion is documented in a simple spec sheet. If it's not in the spec, it doesn't exist. This protects both of us from scope creep and "I thought it would include..." conversations later.

  2. Step 2 — I Build the Complete Site on a Private Staging Server

    You get a private link. You can watch progress anytime. No black box. No "trust me, it's coming." You see exactly what I'm building as I build it. You pay nothing at this stage.

  3. Step 3 — You Review Against the Spec

    Test every form. Check every page. Click every button. Run it through Google PageSpeed. You have a full review window (typically 5 business days) before any money moves. If something doesn't match the spec, I fix it. That's the deal.

  4. Step 4 — You Approve, Then I Invoice

    Only after you confirm in writing that the site meets the agreed spec do I send the invoice. Payment is due in USDT, USDC, or BTC at the rate locked at the moment of invoicing. No surprises, no hidden fees, no currency conversion games.
